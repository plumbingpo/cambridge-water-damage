A burst pipe is one of the most common water damage events a property can face, and potentially one of the most destructive. One broken line can put out 4 to 8 gallons every minute, so 240 to 480 gallons an hour may be running through floors, ceilings and walls. Whether the cause is freezing temperatures, aging plumbing, excessive water pressure, corrosion or physical damage, a burst pipe demands immediate emergency response to limit the damage water does to structural materials, flooring, personal belongings and building systems.

Day or night, Cambridge Restoration responds to burst pipe emergencies across Cambridge and the nearby communities. Our restoration technicians can help you locate and shut off your water supply, and we coordinate with licensed plumbers so the plumbing repair is done properly. With the source under control, our crew goes straight to extraction, using truck-mounted machines and portable extractors to clear standing water from each affected area quickly.

Structural drying comes next and covers the wet drywall, the framing, the insulation, the subfloor and the finished flooring above it. Gravity carries the water through walls, floors and ceilings, so damage from one burst commonly shows up in several rooms and on different floors. Hidden moisture leads to mold and a deteriorating structure, so we use moisture detection equipment and thermal imaging to map the water's path and make sure all affected areas get dried.

How to Tell You Need Burst Pipe Repair

Catching these issues early helps you avoid costly problems and protect your property. Watch for these telltale signs that professional help is needed:

  • Visible water spraying or dripping from exposed pipes
  • Unexplained standing water along baseboards or in the middle of a room
  • In a cold snap, exposed lines that look frosted or frozen over
  • Tap water that turns brown, yellow or cloudy
  • Water pressure that falls off abruptly at every fixture
  • Wet spots, bulging or staining on walls or ceilings
  • Damp, moldy odors around sinks, tubs or walls that hide pipes

Why Hire a Professional for Burst Pipe Repair?

Burst pipe water damage often extends far beyond what is visible on the surface. It moves between ceiling joists, inside floor assemblies, beside electrical conduit and down wall cavities, soaking materials you might not find for days or even weeks. By then, mold may already be established in hidden spaces and structural materials may be significantly weakened. With moisture meters and thermal cameras, trained technicians follow the water's path and pinpoint every affected area right away.

Consumer tools cannot compare with the scale of professional drying and extraction equipment. Compared with a shop vacuum, truck-mounted extraction is hundreds of times faster, and compared with a residential dehumidifier, a commercial one removes 10 to 20 times as much moisture. Mold can begin growing in 24 to 48 hours, so this kind of power and speed matters a great deal.

Burst Pipe Repair Prevention Tips

Taking preventive measures can help you avoid costly water damage issues down the road. Follow these tips from our IICRC-certified technicians:

  • Going away in cold months? Keep the thermostat running to protect the plumbing from freezing
  • Insulate exposed pipes in unheated areas such as attics, garages and crawl spaces
  • If your home still has polybutylene or aging galvanized steel lines, replace them before one gives out
  • Everyone in the house should be able to locate and turn the main water valve in a hurry
  • When it freezes, let taps drip and open the cabinets under sinks to let warm air reach the pipes
  • Install water leak detection sensors near water heaters and main supply lines
